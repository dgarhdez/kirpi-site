Kirpi

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Kirpi shows data from your own PostHog account on your phone. The short version: Kirpi does not collect your data, and we have no servers.

What Kirpi does not do

  • There are no Kirpi accounts and no sign-in.
  • Kirpi does not collect, receive, store or sell personal data, analytics or usage data, or identifiers.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics code.

What stays on your phone

  • Your PostHog API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is sent only to PostHog, never to us.
  • Your settings (the projects you added, event names, pinned events, chart style and filters) are stored on your device only.
  • The trial start date is stored in the device Keychain so that reinstalling does not restart the trial.

Who the app talks to

  • PostHog. When you connect a project, the app sends requests directly from your phone to PostHog's API (us.posthog.com, eu.posthog.com or a host you enter), using your key, to read your own analytics. Kirpi only reads. PostHog's own privacy policy governs what it does with those requests.
  • Apple. The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by the App Store. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to payments.

Your choices

You can remove a project, and its stored key, at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes all local data. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete from us.

Children

Kirpi is a tool for people who use PostHog and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.
